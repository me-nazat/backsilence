## 1. Overview & Architecture

The `AnomalyDirector` is a pure simulation layer component that deterministically schedules, telegraphs, activates, and resolves paranormal environmental phenomena across the Poolrooms.

### Core Architectural Invariants
1. **Pure Simulation Layer:** Zero DOM, Three.js, WebGL, or Web Audio dependencies. The simulation steps at 60 Hz via `AnomalyDirector.step(dt, playerPos, playerVel, isInWater, isStill)` and can be fully serialized and restored via snapshots.
2. **Universal Telegraph Law:** Every anomaly must telegraph its arrival for $\ge 5.0\text{ seconds}$ before mechanical activation. Unannounced or sudden hazard activations are strictly prohibited.
3. **Zero Direct Damage Invariant:** Anomalies deal zero direct health damage. All player deaths remain 100% noise-attributable and creature-delivered.
4. **Decoy Acoustic Truth:** Hallucinatory sounds and phantom silhouettes emit **zero true acoustic events** to `AcousticBus` and `SimWorld.events`. The player's HUD noise meter remains flat during phantom cues, providing an observant player with reliable confirmation that the threat is illusory.
5. **Deterministic Seed Integrity:** Every sequence of anomalies is derived from `Rng` with an explicit integer seed. Replays and tests reproduce identical scheduling, timing, and threshold coordinates.
6. **Competitive Mode Isolation:** Anomalies are automatically disabled in competitive modes (`silent_race`, `last_one_dry`, `silent_cup`, `series`) to ensure deterministic leaderboard parity.

## 2. Anomaly Grammar & Mechanics Catalog

### Family 1: Rising Water (`RISING_WATER`)
- **Premise:** Sudden influx of milky-green water submerging previously safe dry tile platforms and catwalks.
- **Telegraph ($\ge 5.0\text{ s}$):**
  - Acoustic: Deep pipe resonance groan (`tide_pipe_groan`) and sub-bass drainage rumble.
  - Visual: Concentric water bubbles and micro-distortions around floor grates and drain pipes.
- **Active Phase ($30\text{--}60\text{ s}$):**
  - Water level rises smoothly by $+0.15\text{ m}$ to $+0.40\text{ m}$ using cubic smoothstep interpolation.
  - Low-lying dry slabs ($0.30\text{ m}$ elevation) become submerged, forcing the player to choose between wading through water (high acoustic risk) or climbing to higher, narrower architectural perches.
  - Monster wading speed remains constant at $2.4\text{ m/s}$.
- **Drain Phase ($15\text{--}25\text{ s}$):**
  - Water drains back to the baseline map elevation with a rushing vortex audio cue.
- **Resolution:**
  - Water offset returns to $0.0\text{ m}$. Increments `waterAnomaliesSurvived` statistic.

### Family 2: Reality Loops (`REALITY_LOOP`)
- **Premise:** Non-Euclidean spatial threshold loop connecting two distant corridor openings, creating an infinite hallway.
- **Telegraph ($\ge 5.0\text{ s}$):**
  - Acoustic: Fluorescent ballast hum elevation and subtle spatial phase phasing.
  - Visual: Photosafe light flicker ($2\text{ Hz}$, luminance dip $\le 0.18$, delta $< 0.25$) on corridor fixtures.
- **Active Phase ($20\text{--}40\text{ s}$):**
  - Two threshold portals ($A \leftrightarrow B$) become active with trigger radius of $2.5\text{--}3.5\text{ m}$.
  - Crossing Portal A instantaneously relocates the player to Portal B preserving forward momentum and camera orientation.
- **Escape Mechanics:**
  1. **Stillness Escape:** Player ceases all movement (`vel < 0.05 m/s`) for $\ge 3.0\text{ consecutive seconds}$. Loop collapses immediately with a high resonant glass chime, awarding the "Stillness" / "Loopbreaker" tracking flag.
  2. **Water Bypass:** Player drops from the dry corridor into an adjacent water channel or trench, bypassing portal thresholds geometrically.
- **Resolution:**
  - Portals deactivate. Increments `loopsEscapedTotal` statistic.

### Family 3: Sensory Hallucinations (`SENSORY_HALLUCINATION`)
- **Premise:** Visual and auditory false flags generated by liminal paranoia.
- **Telegraph ($\ge 5.0\text{ s}$):**
  - Auditory cadence desynchronization (distant irregular water drip pattern).
- **Active Variants:**
  1. `PHANTOM_FOG_FIGURE`: Spawns a seed-pod monster silhouette in far fog ($\ge 12.0\text{ m}$). Holds stationary or slow wade posture; dissolves into vapor when player approaches within $6.0\text{ m}$.
  2. `PHANTOM_ACOUSTIC_LIES`: Plays directional wade and splash audio cues. Emits zero `NoiseEvent`s on `AcousticBus`; HUD noise meter does not react.
  3. `HALLUCINATORY_WALL_TEXT`: Faint liminal glyphs appear on tile walls ("DO NOT RUN", "IT HEARS"), fading after $8.0\text{ s}$.
- **Resolution:**
  - Decoys dissolve upon expiration or proximity. Increments `hallucinationsDismissed` statistic.
